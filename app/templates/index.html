Why CampusFix?

Built For Transparent Campus Facilities

Say goodbye to lost paper registers and neglected complaints. CampusFix establishes accountability across academic and residential facilities.

1-Click Reporting

Easily submit maintenance tickets for electrical, plumbing, network, furniture, or lab apparatus in seconds.

Live Progress Tracking

Follow the exact state of your complaint from Pending intake to In Progress dispatch and final Resolved sign-off.

Priority Triage

Facility admins categorize, prioritize, and assign urgent issues (High, Medium, Low) to maximize campus safety.

DevOps & Cloud Ready

Packaged as a lightweight Docker container, tested via Pytest, and automated via Jenkins CI/CD on AWS EC2.

Simple 3-Step Process

How CampusFix Operates

1
Student Reports Problem

Select category (e.g. Wi-Fi, Electrical), specify precise campus location, and submit problem details.

2
Admin Reviews & Dispatches

Campus maintenance staff prioritizes the ticket and updates status to 'In Progress' upon dispatch.

3
Resolution & Transparency

Once technicians fix the issue, ticket is marked 'Resolved' and student gets instant confirmation.
